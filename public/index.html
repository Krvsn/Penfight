✏️ PEN FIGHT 5

The school desk showdown — now with up to 5 friends online.

Room:

Invite friends using the link or room code. All players can join from different devices.

How to play
Create a room and invite 1–4 friends. The host starts the match. On your turn, drag from your pen toward the direction you want to shoot, then release. A longer drag gives a stronger flick. Knock other pens off the desk. The last pen remaining wins.
